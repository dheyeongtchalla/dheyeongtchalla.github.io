Academic Research Portfolio

Building practical AI perception systems for robotics, autonomous systems, and intelligent sensing.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at South Dakota State University. My research interests are in computer vision for robotics and autonomous systems, distributed and Federated Learning, and multi-agent AI systems.

My recent work focuses on AI-based sensing, automation, and real-world perception pipelines. I have worked on RGB-D phenotyping platforms, semantic segmentation for crop disease analysis, real-time seed identification with GPS-based spatial mapping, and AI-based automation systems.
